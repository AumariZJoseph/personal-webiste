Cove Booking · v1 · shipping · Solo founder

A booking platform for
Caribbean tour operators.

Every operator gets a public booking page on their own subdomain (book.<slug>.cove.com) or custom domain, an owner dashboard, and one hosted-checkout flow that handles time-slot capacity, age-based pricing, WhatsApp reminders, and refunds. Multi-tenant on a single Flask + Supabase stack — no iframes, no JS widget to install: operators paste one link.
