SwiftCove Booking · live · evolving · Solo founder

A booking platform for
Caribbean experiences.

Every operator gets a public booking page on their own subdomain (book.<slug>.cove.com) or custom domain, an owner dashboard, and one hosted-checkout flow that handles time-slot capacity, age-based pricing, email reminders, and refunds. Multi-tenant on a single Flask + Supabase stack — no iframes, no JS widget to install: operators paste one link.
