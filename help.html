Getting started

From tour details to a live booking page.

SwiftCove is built around a simple outcome: a guest should be able to pick the right date and time, see whether there is room, pay, and receive a real confirmation without your team checking a chat first.

What exactly does SwiftCove do?+

SwiftCove gives each experience a public booking page. A guest chooses a date, picks an available time slot, selects their party size, sees the correct price, and pays through O‑Pay. Once payment is confirmed, the booking is recorded against that specific slot and the remaining capacity updates.

It is designed for experiences that have a real operating schedule—sunset sails, excursions, classes, transfers, private charters, and other bookings where the time and number of available places matter.

How do I start a 15-day trial?+

Request a trial from SwiftCove and we will prepare your account for your business. The trial is the time to put a real experience on the page, test the booking journey, and make sure your O‑Pay connection and availability rules match how you actually operate.

There is no need to decide on a long-term plan before you see it working. After 15 days, you can continue for US$35/month if SwiftCove is a fit.

What should I have ready for setup?+

Bring the same information a guest needs before deciding to book: experience names and descriptions, images, prices, adult or child rates where relevant, operating days, start times, duration, maximum capacity, and your cancellation policy.

You will also need your logo and colours for the booking page, the domain you want to use if you have one, and an active O‑Pay account before card payments can be turned on.

Can you move my existing schedule into SwiftCove?+

Yes. Send your current schedule in the format you already use—a website link, a spreadsheet, screenshots, or even a clear list of departures and prices. We use it to set up the experiences, rates, and availability you need rather than asking you to rebuild everything from scratch.

Bookings & availability

Keep the count honest, even when the day changes.

Your availability should reflect the boat, vehicle, guide, weather, and staff you actually have—not a generic calendar that assumes every day runs the same way.

How are dates, time slots, and capacity set up?+

Each experience is configured with the days it can run, the times guests can book, and the maximum number of people allowed in each slot. A 9:00 am reef tour and a 2:00 pm reef tour are separate slots, so their seats are counted separately.

When a paid booking comes in, SwiftCove applies it to the selected slot. Guests see the remaining places before they commit, while you see the same count in the booking view.

How do I block availability for a day or time?+

Use a per-day availability override when your regular schedule should not apply. You can close an entire date, remove a single departure, or reduce capacity for a specific day without changing the normal schedule that follows it.

That is useful when a private charter takes the boat, a guide is unavailable, the weather forces a closure, or you want to hold places back for a group. Guests will only be shown the slots you leave available.

Can I use different prices for adults, children, private tours, or groups?+

Yes. SwiftCove supports shared and private experiences, adult and child pricing, and per-slot capacity. The total is calculated from the guest choices inside the booking flow, so the amount shown at checkout matches the date, time, party type, and party size they selected.

What happens after a guest books?+

Once O‑Pay confirms the card payment, SwiftCove marks the booking paid, applies the seats to the chosen slot, and records it in your bookings. The guest has a confirmed reservation rather than an enquiry waiting for someone to reply.

SwiftCove also sends an automatic email reminder 24 hours before departure, so guests have the details again when they need them.

How do refunds and cancellations work?+

Your cancellation policy is shown as part of the booking experience. When a booking needs to be refunded, it can be handled from the dashboard instead of being lost in an email thread or a group chat.

Set rules that reflect how your experience actually operates—especially where weather, marine conditions, or limited daily capacity affect what is possible.

O‑Pay payments

A local way to take a card before departure.

SwiftCove runs the booking flow. O‑Pay is the card-payment layer behind it, so a booking is not treated as paid until the payment is confirmed.

Why does SwiftCove require an O‑Pay account?+

All card payments on SwiftCove are processed through O‑Pay’s hosted checkout. An active O‑Pay account is therefore required before your booking page can accept cards. SwiftCove does not replace O‑Pay or hold your card funds; it connects the payment result to the right booking and availability slot.

This gives Caribbean experience businesses a local card-payment route without needing a US bank account, EIN, or stateside setup.

What does the guest see when they pay by card?+

After choosing their date, time, and party details, the guest is taken to O‑Pay’s hosted checkout to enter card details. Cards are captured and 3‑D Secure is handled on O‑Pay’s page—not inside SwiftCove.

When O‑Pay confirms payment, SwiftCove receives that confirmation and marks the corresponding booking paid. A guest should not be counted as confirmed merely because they said they would pay later.

Where do the funds go?+

O‑Pay routes settlement to the account connected to your O‑Pay profile. SwiftCove does not receive or store the guest’s card details; it uses O‑Pay’s payment confirmation to keep your bookings accurate.

SwiftCove supports booking pages that settle in XCD, JMD, TTD, BSD, DOP, GYD, and USD, according to the currencies available to your O‑Pay setup.

Website & branding

Let your site tell the story. Let SwiftCove take the booking.

You do not need to turn an existing site into a booking system. SwiftCove gives it a clean, branded place to send guests when they are ready.

How do I add SwiftCove to my website?+

Point the Book now button on your existing website to your SwiftCove booking-page link. There is no plugin, iframe, or JavaScript snippet to paste into your site. The marketing site stays focused on your story; SwiftCove takes over when a guest is ready to choose a slot and pay.

Can the booking page look like my business?+

Yes. Your SwiftCove booking page can carry your logo, colours, and custom domain, so the step between discovering the experience and paying for it feels continuous. The guest is booking with your business, not being dropped into a cluttered third-party marketplace.

Can SwiftCove build a website for my tours too?+

Yes. If you need a proper landing page for your experience business, SwiftCove can build one around your tours and booking page. It is included free for the first six months of a SwiftCove plan—there is no separate web package to buy.

Account & plan

The practical details.

The plan is deliberately simple: SwiftCove gives you the booking infrastructure; your business keeps control of the experience, availability, and guest relationship.

What is included in the US$35/month plan?+

The plan includes unlimited tours and bookings, a branded booking page on your domain, live slot availability, adult and child pricing, private or shared experiences, O‑Pay card checkout, automatic email reminders, and booking management.

There are no per-booking fees and no add-ons needed for the core booking flow. O‑Pay remains the required payment provider for card transactions.

How do I update my business and booking-page details?+

Keep the details guests see accurate as your business changes: your logo, colours, domain, experience descriptions, schedule, prices, capacity, and cancellation policy. These details are what shape the booking page and the rules guests book against.

For changes that affect a future departure—such as closing a date or changing seats—use the availability and booking settings before guests are sent to that slot.

Can I cancel if SwiftCove is not right for me?+

Yes. The trial lets you test SwiftCove with your real schedule before you continue, and the monthly plan can be cancelled at any time. We would rather the system earn its place in your operation than lock you into a contract that does not fit.
